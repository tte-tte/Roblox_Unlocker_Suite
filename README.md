here is the quick guide and precautions so it runs smoothly on your pc:
Setup:
 extract the zip to a folder on your pc (do not run it inside the zip)
 make sure standard Google Chrome is installed on your Windows
 add your accounts into data/accounts.txt (format username:password)
 if you run a large batch of accounts, add your proxies into data/proxies.txt to prevent Roblox from rate-limiting your IP

How to run:
 double-click start.bat.
 the console will start and automatically open the dark dashboard at http://127.0.0.1:8000/ in your browser
 click "Start All Accounts" (or inspect single accounts)
 to stop the suite, just run stop.bat

Important precautions while running:
 do not move your physical mouse while the bot is typing credentials or holding the "Press and hold" button, so your hand doesn't pull the Windows cursor away from the button
 keep your Windows display scale at 100% (recommended resolution 1920x1080).
 when the bot passes the captcha and opens the QR code modal, it will crop and pop up the QR code right in the dashboard for you to scan with your phone/tablet
